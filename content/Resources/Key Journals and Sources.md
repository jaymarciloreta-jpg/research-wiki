## Core rhinology / skull base journals

- *International Forum of Allergy & Rhinology* (IFAR)
- *Rhinology*
- *The Laryngoscope* / *Laryngoscope Investigative Otolaryngology*
- *Otolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery* & *Otolaryngologic Clinics of North America*
- *Journal of Neurosurgery* / *World Neurosurgery*
- *Journal of Neurological Surgery Part B: Skull Base*

## Allergy / immunology (CRS endotypes & biologics)

- *Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology* (JACI) & *JACI: In Practice*
- *Allergy*
- *Annals of Allergy, Asthma & Immunology*

## Emerging-tech / engineering venues

- *International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery* (Int J CARS)
- *Lancet Digital Health* / *npj Digital Medicine*
- IEEE Xplore, arXiv (cs.RO, cs.CV, eess.IV)

## Trials, guidelines & regulatory

- EPOS (European Position Paper on Rhinosinusitis), ARIA-EAACI, AAO-HNS guidelines
- ClinicalTrials.gov · FDA 510(k)/De Novo database
